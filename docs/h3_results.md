# H3 Grid Results (Mode P, Step 7)

**Data:**
- `results/mode_p/h3/`: 30 cell JSONs, `summary.md` and `per_seed.csv`.
- `results/mode_p/h3/h3_analysis.md`, generated by `scripts/analyse_h3.py`.

**Grid:**
- Layouts: overlap (b = 1) and partial synergy (b = 2).
- Delay d ∈ {0, 25, 50}, with horizon 80 at d = 50.
- Enabler start 0 (slack), 5 seeds.
- Methods: VDN, QMIX, COMA, MAPPO, NA²Q and STAS.

**Run:** 30/30 cells with no failures, 87 minutes on 8 workers on the Ryzen 5 7600 + RTX 4060 (STAS on CUDA).

**Truth:**
- overlap: {A, B, C} = 6 and {B, D} = 4, so Shapley shares are 0.2, 0.4, 0.2, 0.2, 0.
- partial: {A, B} = {A, C} = 10 and {A, B, C} = −10.
- Grading uses the *realised* dividends of each test episode, as in the main grid.

In `summary.md` the tables are grouped by bystander count. In this grid b = 1 is the overlap layout and b = 2 is the partial layout.

## 1. Pre-registered H3

> With overlap or partial synergy, some method–configuration pair has individual credit error in the best third but dividend error in the worst third. NA²Q's coalition recovery falls with d. **Falsified if** individual and dividend errors are tightly correlated across all configurations.

The test uses the 18 pairs formed by the coalition methods (VDN, QMIX, NA²Q) × layout × d.

| part | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| best-third L1 with worst-third dividend error | 0 of 6 pairs. Spearman(L1, dividend error) = 0.55, p = 0.018 | ✗ on the literal rule, but **uninformative** (see below) |
| NA²Q recovery falls with d | interaction mass 0.002 / 0 / 0 at d = 0 / 25 / 50; recall 0.42 / 0.41 / 0.38 | ✗: there is no recovery to lose |

**Why the literal test is uninformative.**
- Dividend error is at its maximum in every pair. Its whole range is 1.9993–2.0000: a spread of 0.0007 on a 0–2 scale.
- Both the thirds and the correlation are computed over that spread, so they are rankings of noise.
- The few hundredths below 2.0 occur where a critic converged at all (QMIX; NA²Q at d = 0), which is also where L1 is lowest. That produces the "significant" positive correlation.
- **Conclusion: the experiment cannot separate methods at the coalition level.** H3 is neither confirmed nor meaningfully falsified.

**Recall and precision do not show recovery.**
- Recall is about 0.4 and precision about 0.3 in every pair, including VDN.
- VDN's mixer game has only singleton dividends by construction.
- These scores therefore come from the Jaccard overlap between singletons such as {B} and true coalitions such as {B, D}, not from any recovered coalition.

## 2. Coalition level: every mixer game is additive

| method | interaction mass, estimated (true = 1.00) |
|---|---|
| VDN | 0.0000 everywhere (by construction) |
| QMIX | 0.0009–0.0021 |
| NA²Q | 0.0000–0.0024 |

**The learned mixers are not too weak.** QMIX can represent any monotone interaction, and NA²Q has explicit pair terms. The limit is the read-out.

**Why the read-out cannot see these coalitions.**
- The mixer game g_t(S) = Q_tot(a_S, noop_{−S}) − Q_tot(noop_all) swaps actions *within one step*.
- The planted coalitions act *across steps*: A pulls the lever, then B, C or D finish later.
- At any one step, at most one coalition member is doing the decisive thing, so the per-step game is additive. Summing additive per-step games over the episode stays additive.

This is the coalition-level form of the semantic gap found for H1b (`docs/main_results.md` §3.1): a per-step counterfactual cannot express synergy between actions taken at different times.

**What would test H3 properly.** A coalition read-out defined on the episode, such as removal of a coalition's whole trajectory, as the ground truth uses, applied to a learned episodic model. That is the H5 low-order estimator, not a mixer read-out.

## 3. Individual credit: no method beats uniform credit (POST-HOC)

The uniform-credit baseline gives 1/n to every agent and is scored against the same per-episode truth and matched target. It is not pre-registered.

| layout | uniform L1 (Shapley / LOO) | best method mean | seeds beating uniform, best method |
|---|---|---|---|
| overlap | 0.45 / 0.49 | STAS 0.52 (d = 0, 25); QMIX 0.80; NA²Q 0.80 (d = 0) | STAS 2/5 |
| partial | 0.89 / 0.90 | QMIX 1.01 (d = 0); STAS 1.04 (d = 50) | STAS 2/5 |

**No method's mean L1 beats uniform credit in any of the 36 method × layout × d conditions.** Per seed:
- STAS beats uniform in 2 of 5 seeds;
- QMIX and NA²Q in at most 1 of 5;
- VDN at most 1 of 5;
- COMA and MAPPO never.

**STAS's apparently best overlap score is a coincidence of the layout.** STAS gives every agent about 0.2 (`docs/step7_ports.md`, known effect 2), and the overlap truth is 0.2 for three of the five agents.

**Implication.** On these layouts, L1 below about 0.45 (overlap) or 0.9 (partial) is needed before individual credit says anything about who earned the reward. Future tables should report the uniform baseline next to L1. This also applies to the main grid, where it was not computed.

## 4. STAS ignores delay (POST-HOC)

- STAS's results at d = 0 and d = 25 are identical: per-episode L1 differs by at most 4e-6 over 150 episodes per layout, and the final losses are identical.
- **This is a property of the method, not a bug.** STAS is trained only on the episode's total return, and its inputs are the agents' observations and actions. Neither changes when the payout is delayed but still falls inside the horizon.
- Only d = 50 differs, because it uses horizon 80 and so different episodes.
- So STAS is trivially robust to delay as long as the payout falls inside the episode. The problem statement's H1 prediction that "STAS has lower M but not zero at the largest d" should be read with that in mind.

## 5. Data quality (H0)

| layout | realised = planted | A critical | test return (planted 10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| overlap | 0.53–0.83 | 0.20–0.27 | 10.0 |
| partial | 0.40–0.50 | 0.00–0.07 | 8.2–8.8 |

- The partial-synergy layout is weak data: fewer than half of its test episodes realise the planted structure.
- Grading against realised dividends keeps the scores valid. But the partial layout tests "synergy as it happened", which is noisier than planned.

## 6. Other observations

The main-grid patterns repeat:
- VDN does not converge under delay (final loss about 1.0 vs 0.05 at d = 0).
- COMA's critic loss is high on the partial layout (about 3.5).
- MAPPO's shared advantage again gives L1 ≈ 2.0 and Λ = b/n by construction.
- STAS's training loss stays high (9.6–18), consistent with its 1,500-update budget.

## 7. Plain-terms summary

- **The coalition question could not be answered with these read-outs.** Every method's coalition read-out sees no teamwork at all, because the teamwork in this environment happens across time and the read-out looks at one step at a time.
- **Individual-credit scores look moderate, but none beats giving everyone an equal share.**
- So individual metrics do hide coalition errors here, but only in the uninteresting sense that the coalition read-outs are uniformly blind. A fair coalition test needs an episode-level read-out, which is H5.

## 8. Limitations

- Each layout has one bystander count and one enabler start.
- 5 seeds and 30 test episodes per cell.
- Only the uniform baseline and the STAS check are post-hoc.
- The mixer game is one choice of coalition read-out, with noop as the null action. A different null action would not fix the problem across time steps.
