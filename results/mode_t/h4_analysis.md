## Reading

- Return strongly predicts 1 − L1 (CI above 0.7) for: no method.
- Weakly (point estimate below 0.3) for: qmix, vdn.
- Only methods that earn reward at some checkpoint can be assessed: VDN and QMIX trained with a seeded replay buffer, COMA and MAPPO after behavioural-cloning pretraining (both non-standard protocols).
- Descriptive only: few methods, a seeded (non-standard) training protocol and our own thresholds.
